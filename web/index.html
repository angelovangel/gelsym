Gel

Click the centres of wells A1, A12 and H1. Each band is assigned to the well it ran from, and sized by its migration distance.

Rows are split at empty gaps between bands; fix them in Rows mode. Each row gets its own lanes and its own ladder (by default the lane with the most bands).

Ladder
